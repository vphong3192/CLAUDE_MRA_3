## 2. Breakdown by design

- **RCT:** 2 (TOCCASTAR, Ullah) — both CF; **no AI/LSI/LID RCT in corpus** (key evidence gap).
- **Meta-analysis:** 1 (Ioannou, AI).
- **Prospective cohort/registry/single-arm:** ~14.
- **Retrospective cohort / lesion-level:** ~10.
- **Guidelines/consensus:** 3.

## 3. Coverage by metric & outcome

- **All four metrics covered** (AI 7, LSI 7, CF 6, LID 9). No metric thin at retrieval.
- **Outcomes present:** first-pass isolation, acute reconnection/dormant conduction, conduction-gap
  prediction, 12-mo (and 24-mo) recurrence, procedure/RF/fluoroscopy time, steam pops/esophageal injury.
- **Head-to-head metric-vs-metric:** AI-vs-CF (A002, A003), LID-vs-LSI (A028), CF-vs-LID acute (A025, A027).
  **AI-vs-LSI direct head-to-head: NONE found** → confirmed gap (assumption A7 supported).

## 4. Gaps identified (feed Law-5 limitations + Law-4 controversy)

1. **No RCT for AI, LSI, or impedance-drop guidance** in this corpus — AI/LSI/LID efficacy rests on
   cohort/registry data (GRADE will likely be Low/Very-Low for recurrence; writer hedge per L-002).
2. **No direct AI-vs-LSI head-to-head** (A7 confirmed). Comparisons are cross-study/indirect.
3. **Threshold heterogeneity (G3):** AI 400/550 (Taghji) vs LSI ranging 3.95–6.5 across studies
   (Kanamori, Cai, Katić, Kuo) — limits pooling.
4. **Population (G2):** predominantly paroxysmal-AF, Western/Chinese single-centre cohorts; only Prasad
   (A012) gives explicit US/EU/Japan contrast. **No Vietnamese-specific data.**
5. **Safety underpowered (A8):** steam-pop/esophageal-injury rates reported inconsistently, small Ns.
6. **CF-alone controversy (G1):** TOCCASTAR (noninferior, not superior) + Ullah (acute benefit, no 12-mo
   benefit) + Szegedi (CF NOT discriminative vs LID) support "CF necessary but insufficient" (A3).
7. **Process gaps this run:** no full text retrieved (tool denied); preprint sweep incomplete (tool denied);
   no dedicated ClinicalTrials.gov ongoing-trial sweep. All flagged in the search log.
