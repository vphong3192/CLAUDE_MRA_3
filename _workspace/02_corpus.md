## Preprint Status

No preprints identified. bioRxiv/medRxiv MCP supports category-only search; no relevant preprints found in screened categories. This field is being actively studied but the evidence base is dominated by published peer-reviewed literature and observational registries. The absence of preprints likely reflects the mature nature of the electrophysiology parameter literature (most foundational data published 1991–2023).

## Deduplication Notes

- REF-010: PMID 25946280 (NEJM 2015 results paper) is the correct STAR AF II citation; PMID 22795225 (2012 design paper) excluded.
- REF-018: PMID 34933570 (Bahnson, age subgroup) is correct; PMID 33499668 (Russo, sex subgroup) excluded.
- REF-066 and REF-038: Both cover Bayés syndrome / advanced IAB but are distinct papers (2020 registry vs 2017 review). Not duplicates.
- REF-064 overlaps with REF-063 and REF-038/065 thematically (P-wave indices) but represents a distinct authoritative consensus document; all retained as the field requires multi-source triangulation.
