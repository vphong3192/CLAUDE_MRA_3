## 2. Assumptions (extrapolations made explicit)
- Single-centre NHS general-anaesthesia split (100%/10.2%) generalises (AR-16) — labelled inference.
- High-income cost direction extrapolated toward LMIC — **magnitude unknown**; no Vietnam/LMIC data exist.
- Paroxysmal-RCT efficacy NOT extrapolated to persistent AF (kept as a Very-Low gap).
- Guideline Class I standing for CBA reflects pre-PFA evidence, not a CBA-vs-PFA adjudication (AR-8).

## 3. Confirmed evidence absences (stated, not papered over)
- No Vietnam / Southeast-Asia / LMIC comparative or cost data (3 search formulations, zero hits).
- No direct CBA-vs-PFA quality-of-life (AFEQT/AF6) comparison.
- No completed persistent-AF RCT.
- Newest variable-diameter CBA (CRYO-PULSE 2025) figures not extractable at this date (currency gap).
