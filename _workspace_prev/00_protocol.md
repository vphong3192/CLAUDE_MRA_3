## 1. Research question (structured)

**Primary question:** In patients with atrial fibrillation undergoing radiofrequency catheter
ablation for pulmonary vein isolation, how do the available lesion-quality / dosing metrics —
**Ablation Index (AI), Lesion Size Index (LSI / VISITAG SURPOINT), contact force (CF), and impedance
drop** — compare with one another, and how does **metric-guided ablation compare with
non–metric-guided (conventional) ablation**, with respect to efficacy (durable PVI, freedom from
atrial arrhythmia), safety, and procedural efficiency?

**Secondary questions (decomposed):**
1. Within each metric, what target threshold is associated with best outcomes (e.g., AI ≥550 anterior
   / ≥400 posterior; LSI ~5.0 anterior / ~4.0 posterior; CF ≥10 g; impedance drop magnitude)?
2. Does AI vs. LSI vs. CF-only vs. impedance-drop guidance differ in **first-pass isolation rate** and
   **acute PV reconnection / dormant conduction**?
3. Does metric guidance reduce **clinical AF recurrence** at ≥12 months?
4. Do the metrics differ on **safety** (steam pops, char/thrombus, esophageal thermal injury, PV
   stenosis, tamponade/perforation)?
5. Do the metrics differ on **procedural efficiency** (procedure time, RF/fluoroscopy time, number of
   applications)?
6. How do answers differ by AF phenotype (paroxysmal vs. persistent), by region (e.g., Asian cohorts),
   and by catheter/contact-force technology?

## 2. PICO framing

| Element | Definition |
|---|---|
| **P — Population** | Adults (≥18 y) with atrial fibrillation (paroxysmal, persistent, or long-standing persistent) undergoing **first-time RF catheter ablation with PVI** as the index lesion set. Includes CF-sensing-catheter procedures. |
| **I — Intervention** | Ablation guided by a quantitative lesion/dosing metric — assessed **individually**: (a) **Ablation Index (AI)** — Biosense Webster CARTO/VISITAG SURPOINT; (b) **Lesion Size Index (LSI)** — Abbott EnSite/TactiCath; (c) **Contact force (CF)** alone (force-time integral / FTI); (d) **Impedance drop** (generator or local impedance, e.g., DirectSense). |
| **C — Comparison** | (1) One metric vs. another (head-to-head: AI vs. LSI; AI vs. CF-only; impedance drop vs. AI/CF); (2) **metric-guided vs. non–metric-guided / conventional** (operator discretion, no real-time metric target); (3) high vs. low target thresholds of the same metric. |
| **O — Outcomes** | **Primary efficacy:** durable PVI / acute reconnection, **first-pass isolation**, dormant conduction (adenosine), **clinical atrial-arrhythmia recurrence** ≥12 mo. **Safety:** steam pops, PV stenosis, atrio-esophageal injury/fistula, pericardial effusion/tamponade, perforation, char/thrombus, stroke/TIA. **Procedural efficiency:** total procedure time, RF (ablation) time, fluoroscopy time, number of RF applications, redo rate. |

> Topic is a **comparative/diagnostic-of-quality** question; PICO is the correct frame. No PECO/PIRO
> adaptation needed.

## 3. Inclusion / exclusion criteria

| Axis | Include | Exclude |
|---|---|---|
| **Study design** | RCT; prospective/retrospective cohort; registry analyses; meta-analysis / systematic review; large case series reporting metric thresholds and outcomes | Single case reports; narrative editorials with no primary data; animal-only or ex-vivo–only studies (unless used solely for threshold-mechanism context, flagged as such) |
| **Population** | AF patients undergoing **RF**-PVI | Cryoballoon-only, pulsed-field-ablation (PFA)–only, or laser-balloon cohorts (no thermal-RF metric); non-AF ablation (VT, atrial flutter without PVI); pediatric |
| **Intervention** | Procedures using/measuring AI, LSI, CF, or impedance drop | RF ablation with no lesion-metric measurement or comparison |
| **Comparator** | Metric vs. metric, metric-guided vs. conventional, or threshold vs. threshold | Studies with no comparative arm AND no threshold–outcome analysis |
| **Outcomes** | Reports ≥1 efficacy, safety, or efficiency outcome above | Surrogate-only with no clinical/procedural outcome |
| **Date** | **2015-01-01 to 2026-12-31** (AI/LSI era; CF catheters matured ~2014–15) | Pre-2015 (pre-dates AI/LSI standardization) — except landmark CF trials (SMART-AF, TOCCASTAR, EFFICAS I/II) cited as historical context, flagged |
| **Language** | English full text or English abstract with extractable data; **actively seek Asian/Vietnamese-relevant cohorts** | Non-English with no English abstract and no extractable numeric data (record as language-coverage limitation per Law 5) |
| **Publication type** | Peer-reviewed primary/secondary; **society guidelines/consensus**; preprints and trial-registry records (tagged not-peer-reviewed per L-004) | Predatory/non-indexed venues; conference abstracts only when superseded by full publication |

## 4. Evidence hierarchy expectations

Anticipated corpus composition for this topic (ranked per Law 3):

1. **Meta-analyses / systematic reviews** — several exist on AI-guided PVI and on CF; fewer direct
   AI-vs-LSI pooled analyses (likely a **methodological/evidence gap**).
2. **RCTs** — present and central: e.g., AI-guided vs. conventional (PRAISE/CLOSE-protocol–derived
   trials), CF-guided vs. non-CF (SMART-AF, TOCCASTAR), low- vs. high-power AI strategies. Direct
   **head-to-head AI-vs-LSI RCTs are expected to be sparse** → flag.
3. **Large prospective cohorts / multicenter registries** — VISTAX, CLOSE, and impedance-drop /
   local-impedance cohorts.
4. **Retrospective cohorts / single-center series** — abundant; main source of threshold-tuning data;
   high heterogeneity in thresholds (anticipated methodological gap).
5. **Preprints (bioRxiv/medRxiv)** — possible for PFA-vs-RF and local-impedance work; downgrade per Law 3.
6. **Trial registries (ClinicalTrials.gov)** — ongoing AI/LSI/impedance trials = evidence-pipeline signal.
7. **Guidelines / expert consensus** — HRS/EHRA/ECAS/APHRS/SOLAECE catheter-ablation documents.

> GRADE will be applied **per outcome** by the appraiser (RoB 2 for RCTs, ROBINS-I / Newcastle-Ottawa
> for non-randomized). Threshold-tuning retrospective data will likely grade **Low/Very Low** for
> efficacy conclusions → writer must hedge language per L-002.

## 7. Curiosity budget — gap-targeted searches (≥3)

| # | Gap type | Targeted search | What it probes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **G1** | **Contradiction** (CF-only vs AI; LSI vs AI) | `("contact force" AND "ablation index") AND (discordant OR "not predictive" OR "no difference")` + direct AI-vs-LSI head-to-head string | Whether CF alone is insufficient vs. integrated AI/LSI; whether AI and LSI give concordant or conflicting thresholds. Feeds the Law-4 "controversy" section. |
| **G2** | **Population** (Asian / Vietnamese / persistent vs paroxysmal / elderly) | `("ablation index" OR "contact force") AND ("Asian" OR "Japan" OR "Korea" OR "China" OR "Vietnam") AND "atrial fibrillation"` ; and `AND ("persistent atrial fibrillation")` | Whether thresholds validated in Western paroxysmal-AF cohorts transfer to Asian / persistent-AF / elderly populations. Directly relevant to a Vietnamese audience. |
| **G3** | **Methodological** (threshold heterogeneity) | `("ablation index" OR LSI) AND (threshold OR target OR cutoff OR "posterior wall" OR "anterior") AND ("atrial fibrillation")` | Quantifies how much study-to-study target values diverge (esophagus-protective posterior-wall lowering, etc.) — a heterogeneity/comparability gap that limits pooling. |
| **G4** | **Evidence gap** (RCT scarcity / follow-up length) | ClinicalTrials.gov ongoing + PubMed `AND (randomized OR RCT) AND ("ablation index" OR "lesion size index")` | Identifies whether high-tier RCT evidence exists for each metric or only for AI; flags short follow-up (<12 mo) as an imprecision/indirectness source for GRADE. |
| **G5** | **Implementation** (real-world workflow / PFA disruption) | `("ablation index" OR "local impedance") AND (workflow OR "real-world" OR registry OR "single-pass" OR "high power short duration" OR HPSD)` ; plus a scan for `pulsed field ablation` to contextualize whether RF-metric work is being superseded | Whether metric-guided protocols integrate into routine labs (HPSD, single operator), and how PFA's rise reframes the relevance of RF lesion metrics. |

## 8. Assumption register (pre-populated — to be confirmed/refuted by the corpus)

| # | Assumption | Status |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | **AI anterior target ≈ ≥500–550** and **posterior ≈ ≥400** (CLOSE protocol) is the most-studied cutoff. | To verify |
| A2 | **LSI ≈ 5.0 anterior / 4.0 posterior** is the most-cited target; LSI evidence base is **smaller** than AI's. | To verify |
| A3 | **CF alone is necessary but insufficient** — integrated indices (AI/LSI/FTI) predict durable lesions better than instantaneous CF. | To test (G1) |
