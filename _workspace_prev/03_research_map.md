## 4. Research gaps (5 types)
- **Evidence gap:** no RCT with a *true* primary-prevention (no-CVD), non-diabetic population reporting MACE — the headline question is answered only by **extrapolation** from secondary-prevention trials and mixed-population meta-analyses.
- **Contradiction gap:** meta-analyses report MACE benefit (RR 0.75–0.80) yet show **no significant effect on CV death or stroke** individually (C1) — the composite is driven mainly by MI; needs careful, non-overstated framing.
- **Methodological gap:** "primary prevention" is applied loosely; pooled analyses mix established-CVD and risk-factor-only patients, inflating apparent applicability.
- **Population gap:** Asian/Vietnamese non-diabetic data absent; SELECT discontinuation rate (16.6%) raises real-world applicability questions.
- **Implementation gap:** strong trial signal vs cost, access, and tolerability-driven discontinuation in primary-prevention (lower-risk) populations.
