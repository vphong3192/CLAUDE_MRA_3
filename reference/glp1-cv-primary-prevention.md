## Peer-reviewed — pivotal trial
- **R1 · Lincoff AM, et al. Semaglutide and Cardiovascular Outcomes in Obesity without Diabetes (SELECT).**
  N Engl J Med 2023;389(24):2221-2232. PMID 37952131 · DOI 10.1056/NEJMoa2307563.
  RCT, n=17,604; age ≥45, BMI ≥27, **preexisting cardiovascular disease, no diabetes**. Semaglutide 2.4 mg
  vs placebo. Primary MACE (CV death/nonfatal MI/nonfatal stroke): 6.5% vs 8.0%, **HR 0.80 (95% CI 0.72–0.90), P<0.001**.
  Discontinuation for AEs 16.6% vs 8.2%. Mean follow-up 39.8 mo. NCT03574597.
  ⚠️ **Population is SECONDARY prevention (established CVD)** — not primary prevention.

## Peer-reviewed — SELECT secondary analyses
- **R2 · Ryan DH, et al. Long-term weight loss effects of semaglutide in obesity without diabetes (SELECT).**
  Nat Med 2024;30(7):2049-2057. PMID 38740993 · DOI 10.1038/s41591-024-02996-7.
  Weight −10.2% vs −1.5% at 208 wk; sustained to 4 y; fewer serious AEs across BMI strata.
- **R3 · Colhoun HM, et al. Long-term kidney outcomes of semaglutide in the SELECT trial.**
  Nat Med 2024;30(7):2058-2066. PMID 38796653 · DOI 10.1038/s41591-024-03015-5.
  Composite kidney endpoint 1.8% vs 2.2%, HR 0.78 (95% CI 0.63–0.96), P=0.02.

## Peer-reviewed — reviews / syntheses
- **R4 · Stumpf MAM, et al. Down the rabbit hole: reviewing the evidence for primary prevention of CVD in people with obesity.**
  Eur J Prev Cardiol 2023;30(17):1895-1905. PMID 37648659 · DOI 10.1093/eurjpc/zwad280.
  Narrative/meta review. States evidence for treating obesity for **primary** prevention is "scarce and
  controversial"; semaglutide primary-prevention signal is **low-grade/extrapolated**; tirzepatide not yet
  tested in CV outcome trials. **Key appraisal anchor for the controversy.**

## Peer-reviewed — meta-analyses (PMID CONFIRMED via PubMed, now citable)
- **R5 · Yin Y, et al. Efficacy of GLP-1RA-based therapies on cardiovascular events and cardiometabolic
  parameters in obese individuals without diabetes: a meta-analysis of RCTs.** J Diabetes 2025;[Epub].
  PMID 40207414 · DOI 10.1111/1753-0407.70082. 29 RCTs, 37,348 pts. MACE RR 0.80 (95% CI 0.72–0.89);
  MI 0.72 (0.61–0.85); all-cause mortality 0.81 (0.71–0.93); **no significant effect on CV death or stroke.**
  ⭐ Key for the **contradiction gap** (composite benefit driven by MI/all-cause, not CV death/stroke).
- **R6 · Ansari HUH, et al. Efficacy and safety of GLP-1 RAs on body weight and cardiometabolic parameters
  in obesity without diabetes: a SR/MA.** Endocr Pract 2023;[Epub]. PMID 38029929 · DOI 10.1016/j.eprac.2023.11.007.
  15 trials. Weight MD −8.77 kg; SBP −4.13 mmHg; DBP −1.39 mmHg; improved lipids (all P<.01). Cardiometabolic
  *parameters*, not hard MACE.
- **R7 · Stefanou MI, et al. Risk of MACE and all-cause mortality under GLP-1 RAs or tirzepatide in
  overweight/obese adults without diabetes: a SR/MA.** Ther Adv Neurol Disord 2024;17. PMID 39345822 ·
  DOI 10.1177/17562864241281903. MACE + mortality meta-analysis in the non-diabetic population.

## Peer-reviewed — foundational efficacy
- **R8 · Wilding JPH, et al. Once-Weekly Semaglutide in Adults with Overweight or Obesity (STEP 1).**
  N Engl J Med 2021;384(11):989-1002. PMID 33567185 · DOI 10.1056/NEJMoa2032183. RCT, n=1961, no diabetes;
  semaglutide 2.4 mg → mean weight −14.9% vs −2.4% placebo. Establishes the weight/metabolic mechanism.
