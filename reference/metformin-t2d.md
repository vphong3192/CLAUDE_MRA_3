Topic: efficacy and safety of metformin in adults with type 2 diabetes (T2D). Captured 2026-06-14.
Source of truth for the writer. Source: PubMed (cite with DOI).

## Guideline
- **R1 · Davies MJ, et al. Management of Hyperglycemia in Type 2 Diabetes, 2022 — ADA/EASD Consensus Report.**
  Diabetes Care 2022;45(11):2753-2786. PMID 36148880 · DOI 10.2337/dci22-0034. (Parallel: Diabetologia
  2022;65(12):1925-1966, PMID 36151309, DOI 10.1007/s00125-022-05787-2.) Person-centred care; greater weight-
  management emphasis; **SGLT2i / GLP-1 RA recommended for cardiorenal protection in high-risk patients
  regardless of metformin/HbA1c** — i.e., metformin no longer the mandatory universal first step.

## Reviews / critical appraisal
- **R2 · Sanchez-Rangel E, Inzucchi SE. Metformin: clinical use in type 2 diabetes.** Diabetologia
  2017;60(9):1586-1593. PMID 28770321 · DOI 10.1007/s00125-017-4336-x. First-line per ADA/EASD; efficacy,
  low cost, weight neutrality, good safety; CV-event reduction "apparently independent of glucose-lowering";
  CKD use restrictions loosened for modest renal dysfunction; "foundation therapy **may justifiably be
  challenged**" by newer agents with stronger cardioprotection evidence.
- **R3 · Type 2 diabetes and metformin. First choice for monotherapy: weak evidence of efficacy but
  well-known and acceptable adverse effects.** Prescrire Int 2014;23(154):269-272. PMID 25954799 (no DOI).
  ⭐ **Critical-appraisal anchor.** UKPDS (~1,700 overweight pts, ~10 y) showed mortality reduction vs diet and
  vs sulfonylurea/insulin, **but methodologically criticised**; a meta-analysis of 10 RCTs showed **no
  statistically significant mortality effect**; ADOPT showed no significant difference in death/CV events.
  Adverse effects: dose-dependent GI, **rare life-threatening lactic acidosis**, vitamin B12 deficiency
  (macrocytic anaemia / neuropathy), rare hypoglycaemia, weight-neutral, no increased cancer mortality;
  renal failure → accumulation. Harm–benefit balance remains favourable.
- **R4 · Echouffo-Tcheugui JB, et al. Diagnosis and Management of Prediabetes: A Review.** JAMA
  2023;329(14):1206-1216. PMID 37039787 · DOI 10.1001/jama.2023.4063. Metformin ↓ progression to diabetes by
  **3.2 cases/100 person-years over 3 y**; most effective in women with prior gestational diabetes and in
  those <60 y with BMI ≥35, FPG ≥110, or HbA1c ≥6.0%; **lifestyle modification > metformin**.
- **R5 · Thrasher J. Pharmacologic Management of Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus: Available Therapies.** Am J Cardiol
  2017;120(1S):S4-S16. PMID 28606343 · DOI 10.1016/j.amjcard.2017.05.009. AACE/ACE + ADA recommend lifestyle
  + monotherapy, **preferably metformin**; choice guided by glycaemic efficacy, weight/hypoglycaemia safety,
  tolerability, cost; context of CVOTs (EMPA-REG, LEADER, SUSTAIN-6).

## Combination-therapy landscape (metformin as backbone) — supportive context
- **R6 · Xie Z, et al. Efficacy and safety of 10 GLP-1 RAs as add-on to metformin: a network meta-analysis.**
  Front Endocrinol 2023;14:1244432. PMID 37701904 · DOI 10.3389/fendo.2023.1244432. 34 RCTs, 12,993 pts.
- **R7 · Xu L, et al. Efficacy and safety of 11 SGLT2 inhibitors as add-on to metformin: a Bayesian network
  meta-analysis.** BMJ Open 2025;15(2):e088687. PMID 40010842 · DOI 10.1136/bmjopen-2024-088687. 23 RCTs, 9,144 pts.
