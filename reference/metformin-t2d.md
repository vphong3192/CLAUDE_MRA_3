### MET-H04 - Griffin SJ 2017 (HIGH)
- **stable_id:** PMID 28770324 / DOI 10.1007/s00125-017-4337-9 ; PMCID PMC5552849
- **title (verbatim, PubMed):** Impact of metformin on cardiovascular disease: a meta-analysis of randomised trials among people with type 2 diabetes.
- **authors / journal / year:** Griffin SJ; Leaver JK; Irving GJ - Diabetologia - 2017
- **source_type:** peer-reviewed (SR/MA of RCTs)
- **retrieved via:** PubMed (P15 known-item) + Consensus C1 | PMID verified by PubMed (Griffin SJ, Diabetologia 2017, DOI 10.1007/s00125-017-4337-9)
- **relevance_tier:** HIGH | **fulltext:** retrieved (PMC5552849)
- **verified finding (captured 2026-10-27):** 13 trials (10 articles), 2079 patients allocated to metformin; only 4 trials compared metformin with placebo and collected CV outcomes (417 patients on metformin). Mantel-Haenszel RR (metformin vs diet/lifestyle/placebo): all-cause mortality 0.96 (0.84-1.09); CV death 0.97 (0.80-1.16); MI 0.89 (0.75-1.06); stroke 1.04 (0.73-1.48); PVD 0.81 (0.50-1.31); none significant. UKPDS weight 52.3% (MI) to 70.5% (stroke). No trial at low risk of bias. Authors: metformin might reduce all-cause mortality by up to 16% but could increase stroke by up to 48%.
- **study_context:** Written for the 60th anniversary series on metformin; previous SRs had doubted metformin's effect on complications, but pooled small, short trials against heterogeneous comparators. Aim: pool RCTs that "isolate" metformin versus diet, lifestyle or placebo, since the FDA has required CV safety data for new drugs since 2008 while metformin evidence is old and thin.
- **study_limitations:** Only 3 databases searched; only trials isolating metformin; heterogeneous CV outcome definitions; inconsistent adverse-event reporting; all included trials at risk of bias or unclear; UKPDS-dominated (342 on metformin, open-label, subgroup analysis, attrition); young, overweight, mainly white North-American/N-European, poorly controlled participants.
- **author_suggestions:** Large long-term pragmatic trials using electronic health records; factorial trials adding metformin to industry trials; publish CV data from adverse-event reporting; a CV endpoint trial of metformin in people WITHOUT diabetes; a placebo-controlled CVOT in T2D judged unlikely.
- **retriever note:** Core of the CV-benefit controversy (UKPDS-era vs modern).
